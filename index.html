    EGRET FREEVERB Reverb

    This browser can't run Egret Freeverb (it needs AudioWorklet). Try a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

    The room in the screen is the reverb: SIZE grows or shrinks it, DAMP (inversely) raises or lowers its ceiling, WIDTH stretches it and spreads the little listener's ears apart. LOW CUT tints the floor and HIGH CUT tints the walls (what gets fed into the tank); TONE washes the room warmer or cooler; the walls light up with the source's sound after a gap set by PREDELAY, by an amount set by MIX. SIZE sets how long the tail takes to decay, DAMP how quickly its high end dies away. Click a knob's value to type one. Space plays the source.
